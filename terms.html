11. Receipt Scanning and Bank Statement Import

The App allows you to scan receipts using your device camera or import documents from your photo library or files for AI-assisted parsing.

Scanning methods:

  • Camera scan: captures a document image directly using your device camera (VNDocumentCameraViewController). Requires camera permission.
