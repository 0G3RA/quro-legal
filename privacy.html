1. Definitions

  • Personal Data — any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.
  • Processing — any operation performed on personal data, including collection, storage, use, disclosure, or deletion.
  • Data Controller — Oleh Herasymenko, the entity that determines the purposes and means of processing personal data.
  • User — any natural person who uses the App.
  • App — the mobile application Quro, available on the App Store.
  • AI Agent — the automated assistant within the App that analyzes financial data and generates responses using Google Vertex AI (Gemini models).
  • Financial Transaction — a record of income or expense manually entered or imported by the User.
  • Bank Statement Import — a feature allowing Users to photograph, scan, or upload bank statements or receipts for AI-assisted parsing.
  • Family Space — a shared account feature allowing multiple users to manage finances together.

3. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account data: collected upon registration via Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In. We collect: display name (from your Apple or Google profile, optional), profile photo URL (from your Apple or Google profile, optional), and email address (stored in Firebase Authentication only, not in our main database)
  • Financial data: transaction amounts, currencies, categories, comments, dates, and tags entered manually or imported by you
  • Budget and goal data: budget limits, savings goals, and scheduled recurring transactions
  • Receipt and bank statement content: image and document data from files you choose to scan or import (see Section 6a)
  • AI chat messages: your messages to the AI assistant and the assistant's responses, including tool call data (transaction lookups, balance queries)
  • Currency preference: your selected primary currency, collected during onboarding

Automatically Collected Information

  • Device information: device model, operating system version, app version
  • Device tokens: push notification tokens (FCM) stored for delivering notifications
  • Crash data: technical crash reports including device info and stack traces, collected via Firebase Crashlytics. A hashed user identifier is associated with crash reports to help diagnose issues; this identifier is cleared upon logout.
  • Usage analytics: app events collected via Firebase Analytics, including: login method, logout, account deletion, onboarding completion, paywall views

Purchase Information

  • Subscription tier, status, renewal date, and transaction history managed via RevenueCat and Apple In-App Purchase

Data Collected Through Device Permissions

  • Camera: used via document scanner (VNDocumentCameraViewController) to capture receipts and bank statements directly. Requires camera permission grant via the standard iOS permission prompt.
  • Photos (via system PhotosPicker): used to select existing photos of receipts or bank statements from your photo library. Uses the system picker which does not require explicit Photos permission.
  • Files: used via system file picker (fileImporter) to select PDF, CSV, Excel (XLSX), JPEG, and PNG files for bank statement import. No special permission required beyond standard file access.
  • Push Notifications: used to deliver reminders and budget alerts. Requested via the standard iOS permission prompt when you first enable reminders in the App.

We do not collect location data, advertising identifiers (IDFA), or any tracking data.

We do not collect special categories of personal data (health data, biometric data for identification, political opinions, etc.).

Our Service is not intended for children under 13 (or 16 in the EU), and we do not knowingly collect their data. If we learn that we have collected personal data from a child without verifiable parental consent, we will take steps to delete that information promptly.

6a. Third-Party AI Services (Google Vertex AI)

To provide AI-powered features (financial chat assistant, receipt scanning, bank statement import), we send certain data to Google Vertex AI.

AI features are powered by Google Vertex AI (Gemini models). All AI processing occurs within the European Union (europe-west1 region). Google does not use your data to train or improve its models. Data is processed in accordance with Google Cloud's Data Processing Addendum and is not retained by Google after processing.

Consent: Before you use AI features for the first time, the App displays a consent screen clearly explaining what data will be sent to Google Vertex AI and for what purpose. AI features are only activated after you explicitly confirm your consent. You may withdraw this consent at any time in App Settings, which will disable AI features while all other App functionality remains available.

Data we send to Google Vertex AI

For receipt and bank statement scanning:

  • The captured image or uploaded document (PDF, CSV, Excel, JPEG, PNG) as base64 data
  • OCR text pre-extracted on your device as a processing hint
  • Your category names and types (to improve categorization accuracy)
  • Language hint

For AI chat assistant (via tool calls based on your queries):

  • Your chat messages and conversation history (up to ~32,000 characters of recent context)
  • Financial data relevant to your query only: transaction descriptions, amounts, dates, categories, account names, budget limits, scheduled payments, currency rates
  • Session context: currency preference, timezone, locale

Data we do NOT send to Google Vertex AI

  • Your name or email address
  • Firebase User ID or Account ID
  • Device identifiers
  • Your full transaction history — only data directly relevant to your query is retrieved and sent

Privacy masking: In Family Space, other members' individual data is masked before being sent to AI.

AI features are optional. The free tier provides full access to manual transaction entry, budgets, accounts, and scheduled payments without using AI. AI features (chat assistant, receipt scanning, bank statement import) require a paid subscription.

7. Data Storage and Retention

All user data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore within the European Union (eur3 multi-region Europe / europe-west1, Belgium).

Categories of data stored on our servers

  • User profile (display name, profile photo URL, currency and language preferences)
  • Financial transactions, accounts (bills), budgets, categories, and scheduled plans
  • AI chat history (messages, tool call results, and generated artifacts)
  • Receipt scan results (extracted text and structured transaction data)
  • Push notification tokens
  • Subscription status and AI usage records (token count, duration — no personal content)

Receipt and bank statement images and documents are not stored on our servers. They are processed in memory during Cloud Function execution and immediately discarded. Only the structured result (parsed transactions) is saved to Firestore.

Retention period: Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. Upon account deletion, all personal data is permanently and irreversibly deleted immediately — there is no grace period or recovery option (see Section 11).

Anonymized data: Anonymized crash reports and aggregated analytics data collected by third-party services (Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Analytics) may be retained after account deletion, as they cannot be linked back to you personally.

Third-party retention: Crash reports are retained for 90 days. Analytics data is retained according to Firebase Analytics default retention settings.

11. Account Deletion

You may delete your account at any time from Settings → Account Settings → Delete Account. Deletion requires re-authentication for security.

Upon account deletion, the following is permanently and irreversibly deleted:

  • All personal financial data: transactions, accounts (bills), categories, budgets, scheduled payments
  • AI chat history, receipts, AI usage records
  • User profile and settings
  • Firebase Authentication record
  • Subscription status record

Deletion is immediate. There is no grace period or recovery option.

Active subscriptions managed by Apple are not automatically cancelled upon account deletion. You must cancel your subscription separately via iOS Settings → Subscriptions.

If you are a member (not owner) of a Family Space: your membership is removed automatically upon account deletion. Shared data you contributed (transactions, bills) remains in the Family Space as it belongs to the shared account, not to you individually.

If you are the owner of a Family Space: you must delete the Family Space before deleting your account (see Section 12).

After deleting your account, you may create a new account and restore your active subscription via Restore Purchases in the App. Previously entered personal data cannot be recovered.

12. Family Space

If you participate in a Family Space, certain data — including transactions, budgets, and account balances — is shared with all members of that Family Space. You must only join or invite others to a Family Space with their explicit consent.

Family Space deletion: The owner may delete the Family Space at any time. Upon deletion, all shared financial data (transactions, bills, categories, budgets, receipts, AI chat history) is permanently deleted and all members immediately lose access. The owner's subscription is not affected and may be applied to a new Family Space.

Member removal: The owner may remove any member at any time. The removed member loses access to the shared account immediately. Data contributed by the removed member remains in the shared account. The removed member's personal account and data are not affected.

12a. Automated Decision-Making (Art. 22 GDPR)

No decisions with legal or similarly significant effects are made solely by automated means. All AI-generated categorizations and suggestions can be reviewed, edited, or rejected by you before saving.
